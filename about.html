About Our Website

About FLAMES Calculator

A simple online tool that brings the traditional FLAMES name game to your browser. Explore the game, understand its rules, and enjoy a playful result with someone you know.

Why We Created This Calculator

The FLAMES game has long been a familiar activity among friends and classmates. People write down two names, compare their letters, and follow a counting process to reach one of six possible results.

We created this website to make that process easier to try online. Instead of manually crossing out letters and counting each round, visitors can enter two names and use the calculator to generate a result.

How the FLAMES Game Works

The traditional version of FLAMES compares the letters in two names. Matching letters are removed according to the game's rules, and the remaining letter count is used in a repeated counting process through the word FLAMES.

Letters are eliminated one by one until a single category remains. Our calculator is designed to make this familiar activity quicker and easier to play.

F — Friends

Represents friendship in the traditional game.

L — Lovers

Represents a romantic connection in the game.

A — Affection

Represents care or fondness in the traditional result.

M — Marriage

Represents marriage as a playful game category.

E — Enemies

Represents the enemies category in the game.

S — Siblings

Represents a sibling-like relationship in the game.

Who Can Use FLAMES Calculator?

The calculator is intended for people who want to try the classic name game for fun. You can use it with a friend's name, a classmate's name, or any two names you want to compare as part of the game.

The result is based on the game's letter-counting rules. It does not require personal relationship details or prove anything about the people whose names are entered.

A Simple and Accessible Experience

Our aim is to keep the experience straightforward. Visitors should be able to find the calculator easily, understand the basic rules, and use the tool without unnecessary steps.

For information about how website data is handled, please read our Privacy Policy .

Entertainment, Not a Compatibility Test

FLAMES is a traditional recreational game, not a scientific method for measuring love, personality, or relationship compatibility. A result cannot predict the future or determine how two people feel about each other. Real relationships depend on communication, mutual respect, and individual choices.
