About FLAMES Calculator

FLAMES Calculator is a simple and fun online tool that lets you explore the classic FLAMES name game using two names.

What is FLAMES?

FLAMES is a popular name-based game that has been enjoyed by people as a lighthearted way to explore relationships between two names.

The word FLAMES represents six categories: Friends, Lovers, Affection, Marriage, Enemies, and Siblings.
