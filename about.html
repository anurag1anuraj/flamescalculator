About FLAMES

A simple and fun name-matching game created purely for entertainment.

What is FLAMES?

FLAMES is a popular name-based game that uses the names of two people to generate a relationship result. The game compares the letters in both names and uses a simple counting method to arrive at one of the FLAMES results.

What Does FLAMES Mean?

F — Friends

A friendship relationship.

L — Lovers

A romantic relationship.

A — Affection

A relationship involving affection.

M — Marriage

A marriage-related result.

E — Enemies

The traditional enemies result.

S — Siblings

A sibling relationship.

How Does It Work?

To play FLAMES, enter two names and start the calculation. The game compares the letters in the names, removes matching letters, counts the remaining letters, and uses that number to eliminate letters from the word "FLAMES".

The process continues until one category remains. That category becomes the final FLAMES result.

Just For Fun

FLAMES is a traditional entertainment game and is not a scientific or psychological compatibility test.

The results are generated using a name-based calculation and should be treated purely as entertainment. They should not be used to make important decisions about relationships or personal life.

Our Goal

Our goal is to provide a simple, fast, and enjoyable FLAMES experience that anyone can use without creating an account or going through a complicated process.

Enter two names, calculate the result, and have fun!
