What is FLAMES?

FLAMES is a traditional entertainment game that uses the names of two people to generate a playful relationship result. It is commonly played among friends and is especially popular as a fun way to see what happens when two names are compared.

What does FLAMES stand for?

F — Friends 👬

L — Lovers ❤️

A — Affection 💕

M — Marriage 💍

E — Enemies 😈

S — Siblings 👨‍👩‍👧

How is the game played?

The traditional method compares the letters in two names. Matching letters are removed, and the remaining number is used to eliminate letters from the word FLAMES in a circular counting process.

Is FLAMES actually accurate?

No. FLAMES is a game and does not have a scientific method for predicting relationships. The result is intended purely for entertainment.
