What Is FLAMES? How to Play the FLAMES Game

Learn what the classic FLAMES game means, how it works, and how you can play it manually with two names.

❤️ What Is FLAMES?

FLAMES is a popular name-based relationship game that people have played for fun with friends, crushes, classmates, and partners. The game uses two names and produces one of six playful relationship categories.

The word FLAMES represents six different categories:

👯

F — Friends

A friendly relationship.

❤️

L — Lovers

A playful love connection.

💕

A — Affection

Care and affection.

💍

M — Marriage

A playful marriage result.

😈

E — Enemies

A playful conflict result.

👨‍👩‍👧

S — Siblings

A sibling-like connection.

🔥 How to Play FLAMES

Playing FLAMES is simple. You only need two names and a few steps to find the result. Here is the traditional way to calculate it manually.

1

Enter Two Names

Choose two names for the game. For example, ANU and RAHUL.

2

Remove Common Letters

Compare the two names and remove letters that appear in both names. Continue until there are no matching letters left.

3

Count the Remaining Letters

Count all the letters that remain after removing the matching letters from both names. This number will be used for the FLAMES calculation.

4

Start With FLAMES

Write the six letters: F L A M E S. Use the remaining-letter count to eliminate letters one by one.

5

Continue Eliminating

Count through the remaining FLAMES letters repeatedly, removing the letter where the count ends. Continue until only one letter remains.

6

Find Your Result 🎉

The final remaining letter represents your FLAMES result. Match that letter with its meaning and discover your playful relationship result.

📝 Simple FLAMES Example

Suppose you want to play FLAMES using the names ANU and RAHUL.

Name 1: ANU

Name 2: RAHUL

Compare the letters and remove the matching letters.

Then count the remaining letters and use that number to eliminate letters from FLAMES.
