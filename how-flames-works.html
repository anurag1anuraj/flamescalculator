How Does FLAMES Calculate Results?

Understanding the classic FLAMES calculation.

Step 1: Enter Two Names

Start with the names of the two people you want to compare. Spaces and punctuation are normally ignored during the calculation.

Step 2: Compare the Letters

The letters appearing in both names are matched. Matching occurrences are removed from both names. The letters left over are then counted.

Step 3: Count the Remaining Letters

Suppose the combined number of unmatched letters is seven. The number seven is then used in the next stage of the FLAMES calculation.

Step 4: Eliminate FLAMES Letters

The letters F, L, A, M, E and S are arranged in a circle. Starting from the beginning, the remaining number is counted and the selected letter is removed. The process continues until only one letter remains.

Step 5: Get the Result

The final remaining letter represents the result according to the traditional FLAMES game.

The calculation is a traditional entertainment game and should not be considered a scientific relationship test.
