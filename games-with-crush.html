Fun Games to Play With Your Crush

Looking for a fun way to spend time with your crush? Try these simple games that can make conversations more interesting and entertaining.

Published September 2026 · 5 min read

Advertisement

💑

Make Time Together More Fun

Talking with someone you like can sometimes feel exciting and awkward at the same time. A simple game can make the conversation more relaxed and give both people something fun to talk about.

You don't need complicated apps or equipment. Many of these games can be played through chat, during a call, or when you are spending time together.

1. FLAMES ❤️

FLAMES is one of the classic name-based games. Enter two names and follow the traditional letter elimination process to get a playful relationship result.

The result can be something like Friends, Lovers, Affection, Marriage, Enemies or Siblings.

2. Would You Rather? 🤔

Take turns asking each other "Would you rather?" questions. Give two interesting choices and let the other person choose one.

Try questions like:

  • • Would you rather travel or stay home?
  • • Would you rather watch a movie or play a game?
  • • Would you rather have unlimited money or unlimited travel?

3. Two Truths and a Lie 🎭

Tell three statements about yourself. Two should be true and one should be false. Your crush has to guess which statement is the lie. It's a simple way to learn unexpected things about each other.

4. Guess the Song 🎵

Send a short lyric, emoji combination, or a few seconds of a song and ask the other person to guess the song. You can also make it more interesting by choosing songs that remind you of a particular memory.

5. This or That ⚡

This is a quick game where you give two choices and the other person has to pick one.

☕ Coffee or Tea?
🌊 Beach or Mountains?
🎬 Movie or Series?
🌙 Night or Morning?

6. Emoji Guessing Game 😍

Use emojis to describe a movie, song, food, place or activity. The other person has to guess what you're describing.

7. Ask Me Anything 💬

Take turns asking questions. Start with easy questions about hobbies, favorite movies, music, travel and food. As the conversation becomes more comfortable, you can ask more meaningful questions.

Tips for Playing With Your Crush 💕

❤️

Keep the game fun and relaxed.

😊

Don't pressure the other person to answer uncomfortable questions.

💬

Use the games as conversation starters.

✨

Most importantly, enjoy the conversation.

Final Thoughts

You don't need an elaborate plan to have a good time with someone you like. A simple game can break the ice, create funny moments and help you discover common interests.

Whether you choose FLAMES, a guessing game or simple questions, remember that the goal is to have fun and enjoy each other's company.

❤️ A little reminder

Games like FLAMES are intended for entertainment. They cannot scientifically determine whether two people are compatible or predict the future of a relationship.
