How Does the FLAMES Game Work?

The traditional FLAMES game uses the names of two people to calculate an outcome. First, players write down both names and compare their letters. Matching letters are removed according to the game's rules, and the remaining letters are counted. This count is then used to eliminate letters from the word FLAMES through a repeated counting process. The elimination continues until only one letter remains. That final letter determines the result displayed by the game. An online FLAMES calculator performs these steps automatically, making the process faster and easier without requiring manual counting.
