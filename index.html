About FLAMES

What is the FLAMES Game?

FLAMES is a classic name-based game that has been enjoyed as a fun activity for many years. The word FLAMES represents six possible results: Friends, Love, Affection, Marriage, Enemies, and Siblings.

Our FLAMES calculator lets you enter two names and calculates a result using the traditional FLAMES game method. The calculation is based on the letters contained in the names.

FLAMES is intended purely for entertainment. The result should not be considered a scientific measurement of compatibility or a prediction about a real relationship.

Simple Process

How Does FLAMES Work?

The traditional FLAMES calculation uses the letters in two names to produce a fun final result.

✍️

1. Enter Two Names

Enter the names of two people into the FLAMES calculator.

🔤

2. Match the Letters

Common letters between the two names are matched and removed. The remaining letters are counted.

❤️

3. Get Your Result

The remaining count is used with the FLAMES sequence until one final letter remains.

No. FLAMES is a recreational name game and is not a scientifically validated compatibility test.
