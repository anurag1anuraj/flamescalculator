How to Calculate FLAMES Manually Without a Calculator

Learn how to calculate the classic FLAMES game by hand using two names, matching letters, and the circular elimination method.

Have you ever wondered what your relationship result would be with a friend, crush, or someone special? The FLAMES game is a classic name-based game that people have enjoyed for years.

You don't actually need an online calculator to play FLAMES. You can calculate the result yourself using just two names, a pen, and a piece of paper.

In this guide, we'll show you how to calculate FLAMES manually step by step.

What Is the FLAMES Game?

FLAMES is a fun name-matching game. The letters in FLAMES traditionally represent:

F – Friends
L – Love
A – Affection
M – Marriage
E – Enemies
S – Siblings

The game uses the letters in two names to produce one of these six results.

Note: FLAMES is an entertainment game and should not be considered a scientific or accurate way to predict a relationship.

How to Calculate FLAMES Manually

The basic process has four steps:

  1. Write down the two names.
  2. Remove matching letters from both names.
  3. Count the letters that remain.
  4. Use that number to eliminate letters from FLAMES.

Let's go through an example.

Step 1: Choose Two Names

For example, let's use:

Name 1: ARUN

Name 2: ANU

Write the names separately.

Step 2: Remove Matching Letters

Now compare the letters in both names.

A R U N

A N U

The letters A, N, and U appear in both names, so we remove those matching letters.

After removing them:

ARUN → R

ANU → nothing

The remaining letter count is:

1

So our final count is 1.

Step 3: Write FLAMES

Now write the six FLAMES letters:

F L A M E S

We use the remaining-letter count to start eliminating letters.

For our example, the count is 1.

1 → F

So F is eliminated.

The remaining letters are:

L A M E S

Step 4: Continue the Elimination

This is the important part of the FLAMES game.

After removing one letter, continue counting from the next letter rather than starting from F again.

When you reach the end, continue again from the beginning.

Keep repeating this process until only one letter remains.

Step 5: Check the Final Letter

The last remaining letter determines the FLAMES result.

Letter Meaning
F Friends
L Love
A Affection
M Marriage
E Enemies
S Siblings

For example, if the final letter is L, the FLAMES result is:

❤️
Love

If the final letter is F, the result is:

🤝
Friends

Important Rule: Counting Around FLAMES

One of the easiest mistakes is restarting the count from F after every elimination.

Continue counting from the position after the letter you just eliminated.

Once you reach S, continue again from the beginning of the remaining list.

This circular counting process is what makes the FLAMES game work.

Is the FLAMES Result Accurate?

FLAMES is primarily a fun and entertaining name game. The result is generated from a simple letter-counting and elimination method.

It is not a scientific relationship compatibility test .

So whether the result says Friends, Love, Marriage, or something else, treat it as a fun game rather than a prediction of your actual relationship.
